## 7. First 30 Days — Validate

## 8. Days 31–60 — Pilot
- Launch controlled pilot
- Track lead → onboarding → activation → retained supply
- Test incentive structures
- Measure utilization and trip density
- Refine area prioritization
- Close top operational gaps

## 9. Days 61–90 — Scale Decision

- Cut low-performing channels
- Formalize partner model
- Standardize operating SOP
- Confirm 6-month expansion roadmap
- Present scale / no-scale recommendation
